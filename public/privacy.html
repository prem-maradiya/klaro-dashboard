Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Klaro is a personal expense tracker that reads your bank's transaction SMS messages to automatically log what you spend. This policy explains exactly what data Klaro handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your bank messages are read and understood entirely on your phone, and your financial data stays yours — it is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used for any purpose other than showing you your own spending.

1. What Klaro reads

With your permission, Klaro reads SMS messages on your device and parses the ones that look like bank or payment alerts. From a matching message it extracts only:

  • the amount and whether it was money in or out;
  • the merchant / payee name and any UPI ID present;
  • the date and time of the transaction.

The original message text is kept on your device only (so duplicate alerts aren't counted twice) and is never uploaded. Messages that are not transaction alerts (personal texts, OTPs, promotions) are ignored and never stored. Klaro does not send your SMS inbox anywhere.

2. What stays on your device, and what syncs

Every bank message is read and parsed entirely on your device. The raw SMS text, your account numbers, your balance and UPI IDs never leave your phone — there is no cloud parsing and no AI.

So that your data appears on the companion web dashboard and can be restored if you reinstall, Klaro syncs only the cleaned transaction list to its backend:

  • merchant / payee name, amount, category, and date;
  • a one-way hash of the message, used solely to avoid counting the same transaction twice — it cannot be reversed back into the original message.

The raw message text, account numbers and balances are never uploaded. This cleaned data is stored on Klaro's server so it can be shown back to you on your phone and the web dashboard; it is used only to run the app for you, and is never sold or shared. You can delete it at any time — see the Data Deletion page.

3. Permissions and why they're needed

  • SMS (read / receive): the core feature — to detect and parse bank transaction alerts as they arrive and when you run a catch-up scan. This is the only way Klaro can log spending automatically.
  • Contacts (optional): only to show a friendly name when a payment is to or from someone in your contacts. Reads names locally; never uploads your contacts.
  • Notifications: to alert you when a new transaction is captured or a budget threshold is reached.

4. How categorization works

Klaro guesses a category (Food, Transport, …) using a fixed built-in list of known merchants and keywords, plus what you've taught it by correcting past entries. This uses only the merchant name (which is already part of your transaction) — no AI service and no third party is ever involved, and your message text is never used for it.

5. What Klaro never does

  • Never sends your raw SMS, account numbers, balances or UPI IDs off your device.
  • No AI service or other third party ever processes your messages.
  • No selling or renting of your data — ever.
  • No advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

6. Your control

You can delete any transaction inside the app, revoke the SMS or Contacts permission at any time in your device settings, or uninstall Klaro to remove all on-device data. If you use a backend, you control that server and can clear it directly.

7. Your account

Klaro uses an account (email + password, or Google sign-in) so your data is private to you and available across your phone and the web dashboard. We store your email, an encrypted authentication record, and the cleaned transactions you sync (section 2) — nothing more. You can delete your account and all data at any time; see the Data Deletion page.

8. Children

Klaro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
