Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 June 2026

Klaro is a personal expense tracker that reads your bank's transaction SMS messages to automatically log what you spend. This policy explains exactly what data Klaro handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your financial data stays yours — it is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used for any purpose other than showing you your own spending.

1. What Klaro reads

With your permission, Klaro reads SMS messages on your device and parses the ones that look like bank or payment alerts. From a matching message it extracts only:

  • the amount and whether it was money in or out;
  • the merchant / payee name and any UPI ID present;
  • the date and time of the transaction;
  • the original message text, kept so duplicate alerts aren't counted twice.

Messages that are not transaction alerts (personal texts, OTPs, promotions) are ignored and never stored. Klaro does not send your SMS inbox anywhere.

2. Where your data is stored

Transactions are stored on your device. If you choose to connect a backend (the optional sync server you configure in Settings), your transactions are also sent to that server, which you control, so the same data appears on the companion web dashboard. Klaro has no shared central database — there is no Klaro account, and the developer cannot see your data.

3. Permissions and why they're needed

  • SMS (read / receive): the core feature — to detect and parse bank transaction alerts as they arrive and when you run a catch-up scan. This is the only way Klaro can log spending automatically.
  • Contacts (optional): only to show a friendly name when a payment is to or from someone in your contacts. Reads names locally; never uploads your contacts.
  • Notifications: to alert you when a new transaction is captured or a budget threshold is reached.

4. Optional AI categorization

To suggest a category for an unfamiliar merchant, Klaro can send the merchant name only (e.g. "Blue Tokai Coffee") to Google's Gemini API. No amounts, dates, account numbers, or message text are sent, and this only happens for merchants that can't be categorized locally. If no AI key is configured, this feature is simply off.

5. What Klaro never does

  • No selling or renting of your data — ever.
  • No advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No sharing of your transactions with anyone other than the backend you set up.

6. Your control

You can delete any transaction inside the app, revoke the SMS or Contacts permission at any time in your device settings, or uninstall Klaro to remove all on-device data. If you use a backend, you control that server and can clear it directly.

7. Your account

Klaro uses an account (email + password, or Google sign-in) so your data is private to you and available across your phone and the web dashboard. We store your email and an encrypted authentication record — nothing more about your identity. You can delete your account and all data at any time; see the Data Deletion page.

8. Children

Klaro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
