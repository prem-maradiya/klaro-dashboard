Data Deletion

Last updated: 20 June 2026

You can delete your Klaro data at any time. This page explains how, and what gets removed — as required by the Google Play data-safety policy and India's DPDP Act.

Option 1 — Delete everything (your account)

To permanently delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Email premmaradiya333@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my Klaro account"; or
  2. use the in-app Settings → Delete account option (where available).

We will permanently delete your account and all your transactions, budgets, categories, and settings within 7 days, and confirm by email. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Delete individual data

  • A single transaction: open it in the app or web dashboard and choose Delete.
  • On-device data only: uninstall the app to remove everything stored locally on your phone.
  • Stop SMS access: revoke the SMS permission in your phone's Settings — Klaro will stop reading new messages immediately.

What is deleted

Account deletion removes: your transactions (amounts, merchants, dates, and the original SMS text used to parse them), budgets, learned categories, recurring dismissals, and your account record. Backups are purged on their normal rotation (within 30 days).

What may remain briefly

Routine encrypted backups may retain a copy for up to 30 days before they cycle out. Anonymous, non-identifying logs (e.g. error counts) are not linked to you.
