Help & FAQ

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Contact us below.

How does Klaro know my spending?

It reads your bank's transaction SMS on your phone (with your permission) and extracts the amount, merchant, and date. Personal texts and OTPs are ignored.

A transaction is missing or wrong — what do I do?

Open ⋮ → Import from SMS inbox to re-scan for anything missed. To fix a category or name, tap the transaction and edit it — Klaro remembers your choice for that merchant next time. You can also add a payment manually with the + button.

Can I use Klaro without giving SMS access?

Yes — you can add transactions manually. Automatic capture just won't work without SMS permission.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is tied to your account, isolated from other users, never sold, and never shared with advertisers. See the Privacy Policy and Security page.

How do budgets and alerts work?

Set an overall monthly limit or per-category budgets in ⋮ → Budgets. Klaro notifies you at 80% and again when a budget is exceeded.

What are "Upcoming payments"?

Klaro detects recurring charges (subscriptions, EMIs, bills) and predicts the next due date. Cancelled one? Swipe it away to dismiss it.

How do I see my data on a computer?

Sign in to the web dashboard with the same account to view, search, edit, and export (CSV) your transactions.

How do I delete my data or account?

See the Data Deletion page — you can remove individual transactions, on-device data, or your whole account.
